Diagnosis from a dozen live walkthroughs: the flags drive the band, but the band is two scrolls and a screen-swap away from the flags. The valuation engine answers in ~190 ms — fast enough to treat the band as a live readout instead of a destination. Three cumulative iterations, each judged before the next.

What's rough

  • The one action lives below seven cards — every run is a scroll hunt (partially patched with scroll-reset, but the reach remains).
  • Flag → result feedback loop is broken by a screen swap: flip a flag, scroll down, tap, read, tap back, scroll up.
  • Confidence — the product's soul — is invisible while curating, exactly when it should be teaching.

What's already right and untouchable: testimony is explicit taps, engine words verbatim, orange = one action, no client math.

The move

  • Once subject SF + one comp exist, the app runs the band itself — debounced ~700 ms after the last change. The engine is pure, free, and answers in 190 ms p50 (stress-tested); the contract literally says "re-run liberally."
  • The sticky bar becomes a live P20 · P50 · P80 readout with the confidence word. Flip a flag → watch confidence move. The honesty stops being a screen you visit and becomes physics you feel.
  • The engine's refusals surface in the same bar, verbatim ("widen the set, not the band") — errors land where your thumb is, not above the fold.
  • The bar's one tap → the full Answer (breakeven, tiers, gap, tabs, PDF) — now an expansion, not a leap of faith.

Rules check

The app still computes nothing — it just asks more often. Never one number: the strip is always the triple + confidence. Testimony still requires deliberate taps; the band reacting to them rewards testimony rather than replacing it.

The move (and why it dies)

  • Skip Curate: run the band on raw unflagged candidates the moment enrichment lands, then ask for flags to "improve" it.
  • Faster to a number — but the wrong ceremony. The first thing every user would see is the NONE-SOLD scream on data they haven't touched, teaching them the app yells before they've done anything.
  • Worse: people anchor on the first number they see. Showing a band built on evidence nobody attested to optimizes for the anchor over the testimony — the exact inversion of what makes this product defensible.

Judgment

Rejected. Iteration 2 already gives the speed (the band appears seconds after landing on Curate if the sqft came from enrichment) without ever showing a number ahead of the user's own attestation… because the auto-run still waits for ready, and unflagged runs honestly carry their low confidence in the strip.
